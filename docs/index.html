Odyssey

Eden Samara

New release on Local Action (with Lapsus).

UKGHouseJungleFootwork

Canadian-born vocalist, producer and songwriter. "Odyssey" (September 4, 2026) is her second Local Action LP -- 10 tracks moving between new age pop, 2-step UKG, house, R&B and footwork-jungle, with co-production from Tim Reaper, Comfort Zone and TSVI, drawing on Homer's Odyssey and The Alchemist as she traces the emotional aftermath of watching loved ones struggle with addiction and mental health.

Why listen: A genre-hopping dance-pop song cycle that's genuinely club-rooted rather than just pop with beats -- useful for a set that wants vocal hooks without losing rhythmic range.
