Pozo de Agua

Le Motel

New label discovery: Constellation Tatsu. Similar ambient/field-recording territory to your Brian d'Souza and Andrew Pekler-adjacent picks.

AmbientField RecordingDowntempo

Brussels-based producer, composer and field recordist. "Pozo de Agua" (September 15, 2026) was largely made outdoors and on trains -- ripples, water drops, movement and air treated with the same weight as instruments, drifting in and out to form subtle polyrhythms and overlapping textures.

Why listen: Immersive, environmentally-sourced ambient that rewards close listening -- ideal for an opening set or a contemplative wind-down.
