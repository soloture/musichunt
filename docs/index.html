New release on Multi Culti (World Records).

Ambient TechnoHouseSound Art

Denis Morin's project, expanding his 'Island' series. "L'Île Sans Frontières" (July 25, 2026) is a five-track EP -- Le Village, Dauphin Dance, Mon Chat a Bali, My Soul Can Sing, Club Nostalgic -- blending ambient techno, house, field recordings, spoken word and dubby textures, featuring auto-tuned voices gathered through workshops with migrant communities.
