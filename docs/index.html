Ladybug 5

The Bug / YL Hooi

The Bug already in your library. New label discovery -- Pressure, Kevin Martin's own imprint. Latest entry in his ongoing Ladybug collaboration series.

DubPost-PunkDancehall

"Ladybug 5" (November 20, 2026, Pressure) pairs Kevin Richard Martin (The Bug) with Melbourne artist YL Hooi (Valya Ying-Li Hooi, of Kallista Kult), the latest in his ongoing Ladybug split-release series following entries with Dis Fig, Friday Night Plans and Hollie Cook.

Why listen: Heavy, dub-weighted production meeting YL Hooi's dreamy, post-punk-inflected vocals -- a good dark, atmospheric change of pace in a set.

Josh Ludlow

Rhythm Section International already seeded. New artist discovery -- a rare solo EP from a member of London duo Make A Dance, with a longtime connection to label head Bradley Zero.

TechnoHouseEBM

"Brain Music" (October 30, 2026, Rhythm Section International, cat. RS080LP) is a four-track EP from Josh Ludlow (of London duo Make A Dance), mixing Detroit techno influences, acid lines and off-centre house rhythms.
