Music To Watch Seeds Grow By 009: Patricia Wolf (Yarrow)

Patricia Wolf

New label discovery: Music To Watch Seeds Grow By (Ransom Note), the same series that gave you Brian d'Souza's Sunflowers.

AmbientField Recording

Portland-based musician and field recordist. "Yarrow" (June 4, 2026) is the label's ninth release, made in response to an artist residency at the Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory studying plants, pollinators and their ecological relationships -- each track reflects a different part of a plant's life cycle, weaving field recordings into the electronic framework.

Why listen: Deeply considered, research-grounded ambient from a strong field-recording composer -- another good option for an opening set or a quiet interlude.
