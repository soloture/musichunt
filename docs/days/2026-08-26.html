ARTIST Label watch Album · Jun 5, 2026

Tayta

Montoya

New release on ZZK Records.

CumbiaAndeanElectronic

Colombian producer on ZZK since 2019. "Tayta" (June 5, 2026) is an 8-track album described as "an inner journey toward deep listening" -- ethereal yet funky cumbia fusing Amazonian and Andean folk roots with melodic electronica, its visual language built around Mexican artist Diego Ocote's chaquira-bead weaving as a metaphor for the album's layered sonic cells.

Why listen: A warmer, more meditative strand of digital cumbia than most ZZK output -- good for easing into or out of a Latin-leaning stretch of a set without losing the folkloric backbone.

Splash of Milk

GUSH

Leftfield/experimental territory similar to Legowelt and DJ Plant Texture already in your seeds; new label discovery (Huntleys + Palmers).

Avant PopKrautrockExperimental

Stripped-down avant-pop duo Steven Warwick (formerly Heatsick) and Ivan Brito (aka Vanya). "Splash of Milk" (April 22, 2026) is their LP on the Glasgow label Huntleys + Palmers, mixing short song forms with a looser, psychedelic approach -- somewhere between Silver Apples-style psychedelia and krautrock, with post-punk humor.

Why listen: Not a straightforward club record, but a good curveball for a set's weirder stretch -- wonky, hypnotic, and rhythmically strange enough to still hold a room.
