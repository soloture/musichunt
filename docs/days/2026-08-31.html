Petrology

Scuba

New release on Hotflush Recordings.

TechnoRaveDub

Paul Rose, Hotflush's own founder. "Petrology" (April 10, 2026) is a six-track, vinyl-only Record Store Day EP -- Homing Device, Puppets, Petrology on side A, three Tío versions on side B -- covering the widest stylistic range of his productions since his 2015 album "Claustrophobia."

Why listen: A producer revisiting the full width of his own language -- techno, rave energy, dub depth and ambient drift in one release, with enough atmosphere to keep it from feeling purely functional.

New release on Delsin Records.

TechnoElectronica

Berlin producer marking his first album in eight years. "Halo" (June 26, 2026) applies the solid structures of floor-focused Berlin techno to the expressive space of introspective electronica.

Why listen: A genuine comeback record -- functional enough for the floor but with more emotional range than straight club techno, good for a set's more introspective stretch.
