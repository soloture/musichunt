"Vidrig vår" (September 25, 2026, Skolhaus) is Swedish psych band Dungen's 12th studio album -- ten songs, their first since 2022's "En är för mycket och tusen aldrig nog," an open, personal account of a long relationship ending, produced by Gustav Ejstes and Mattias Glavå.

Why listen: A different texture entirely from the electronic-heavy picks this week -- warm, melodic Swedish psych rock, useful if a set calls for a live-band interlude or a change of instrumentation.
