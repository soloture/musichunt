New York-based composer, cellist and multi-instrumentalist. "Solar Calf" (July 31, 2026) is his second solo album on PAN, using video-game-music vernacular to build a playable-feeling sonic environment rather than a linear beginning-to-end record.

Why listen: Not a club tool -- a headphones-first, texturally rich listen in the same vein as your Max Cooper/Jack Sheen picks, worth knowing for intros, transitions, or off-the-clock listening.
