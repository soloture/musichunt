TechnoHouseDabke

"Please" (2026, Smalltown Supersound) is the second album from Melbourne producer Jarred Beeler, aka DJ Plead -- built from roughly 120 demos recorded between 2023 and 2025, fusing techno, house and dub with the dabke and mijwiz-flecked keys of a Levantine dancing circle.

Why listen: A genuinely distinct hybrid sound -- Levantine melodic instrumentation folded into club rhythm rather than bolted onto it -- useful for a set that wants a regional texture shift without losing the floor.
