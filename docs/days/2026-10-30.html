Places Remember Events

Eerie Enterprise

New collaborative project from upsammy (already seeded via Dekmantel/PAN) and Piezo. A different, new release from upsammy's April "Seismo" collaboration with Valentina Magaletti.

AmbientExperimentalIDM/Experimental

"Places Remember Events" (October 30, 2026, Dekmantel) is the debut album from Eerie Enterprise -- the joint project of Dutch producer upsammy (Thessa Torsing) and Italian producer Piezo (Luca Mucci) -- nine tracks developed over a year and a half, opening with the title track and "Decay + Collapse."

Why listen: Melodic, ambient-leaning electronica from two distinctive producers working together for the first time -- out today, a strong headphones-first pick for a contemplative stretch of a set.
