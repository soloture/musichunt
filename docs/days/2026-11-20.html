"Energy Exchange" (Up Ya Archives, cat. UYA014) is a four-track EP from Dinn Warde, aka Dwarde -- a Darlington-born, London-based jungle producer and a key figure in the contemporary jungle/breakbeat revival, with two jungle cuts and two brighter, vocal-led hardcore tracks.

Why listen: Big-vocal jungle and hardcore from one of the revival scene's most distinctive voices -- good for an energetic, nostalgic-leaning stretch of a set.
