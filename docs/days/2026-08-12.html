ARTIST Label watch Album · Mar 27, 2026

Nuevos Rios

Nuevos Rios

New album on ZZK Records.

WorldLatin American electronic

Collaboration between Colombia's Canalón de Timbiquí (led by Nidia Góngora, also known from Quantic/Ondatrópica/The Bongo Hop) and Toulouse trio Reco Reco. Self-titled album "Nuevos Rios" (March 27, 2026) is on ZZK Records.

Why listen: Marimba, percussion and voice from Afro-Colombian tradition interlaced with keyboards, guitar and drums -- a hypnotic bridge between West Africa, the Caribbean, and club electronics. Good change-of-pace record, not a straight club tool.
