ARTIST Label watch Album · Jan 22, 2026

Press

Jack Sheen

New release on The Trilogy Tapes.

Contemporary ClassicalExperimental

Composer, sequel to his 2023 "Solo for Cello". "Press" (Jan 22, 2026), performed by ensemble Apartment House (violin, viola, cello, piano), applies the same techniques to a five-piece group across 14 movements.

Why listen: Not a club record at all -- pitchy, kinetic, deliberately unsteady chamber composition. Worth knowing for a set's furthest-out moment, or just as a genuinely different kind of listening.
