LABEL Label watch Compilation · Jun 12, 2026

The Interactive Test Experience Vol.1

Franco Falsini (Various Artists)

New compilation on Space Echo Records.

Library MusicTechnoItalo

Franco Falsini and brother Riccardo's Interactive Test project -- a small creative laboratory born in early-90s Italy where electronic music was treated as a language, not a genre. "The Interactive Test Experience Vol.1" (June 12, 2026) gathers work released under aliases like Open Space, Youth Wave, and Agent Fylfoyt.

Why listen: Atmospheric techno, digital psychedelia and club-focused machine grooves that capture a specific early-90s Italian club/warehouse scene -- deep source material if you like your techno with some archival weight.

ARTIST Similar Album · Jul 17, 2026

Fantasy

Piero Umiliani

Same Italian library/soundtrack lineage as Alessandroni (above), also on a Four Flies imprint.

Library MusicSoundtrackItalo

Legendary Italian library/soundtrack composer (d. 2001). "Fantasy" (July 17, 2026), recorded in 1983 and never released until now, came out on Four Flies on what would have been his 100th birthday -- his last unreleased title intended for his own Telesound library imprint.

Why listen: A genuinely lost chapter surfacing over 40 years later -- rare occasion to hear "new" Umiliani. Great texture/sample source for anyone into library music.
