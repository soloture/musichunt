Corps Perdu

Lamin Fofana

Already in your library (The Trilogy Tapes roster). New album on Shelter Press.

AmbientExperimentalElectronic

"Corps Perdu" (October 16, 2026, Shelter Press) is a ten-track double LP from Lamin Fofana, informed by scholars and poets working in the Black Studies tradition and taking its title from Aime Cesaire's 1950 poetry collection -- tracks include "To Become Liquid" and "Ode to Guinea," following 2025's "Works In Metal."

Why listen: Dense, poetic ambient/electronic work from an artist already in your library -- strong material for intros, warmups, or a contemplative stretch of a set.
