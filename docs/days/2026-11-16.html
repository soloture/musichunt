ARTIST Label watch EP · Nov 6, 2026

Permafrost

ELOQ & V. Christie

Shall Not Fade already seeded -- this lands on the label's Time Is Now sub-imprint (cat. TIN081). Pairing of Copenhagen producer ELOQ and Norwegian producer V. Christie.

UK GarageHouseSpeed Garage

"Permafrost" (November 6, 2026, Time Is Now / Shall Not Fade) is a four-track 12" from Danish producer ELOQ (August Fenger Janson) and Norwegian producer V. Christie (Vebjørn Christie), pairing the title track "Coldsnap" with garage/speed-garage cuts "Fools (Wizards Theme)" and "Nightshifter (Assassin's Theme)" plus closer "Anchor."

Why listen: Hands-up UKG/speed garage energy from two scene-connected producers -- built for a peak-time, high-tempo stretch of a set.

ARTIST Label watch Album · Nov 12, 2026

Mara Gram

Azzi On The Beat

Nyege Nyege Tapes already seeded (prior picks: Kazi/Kadodi Season Two). New artist discovery -- his debut album for the label.

Afro-ElectronicStreet PopExperimental

"Mara Gram" (November 12, 2026, Nyege Nyege Tapes) is the debut album from Lagos producer Azzi On The Beat (Waris Rasheed), canonizing mara, a Lagos street-pop dance style, across 18 tracks of ecstatic, unhinged productions. He has previously played MoMA PS1, Tresor, and Unsound.

Why listen: Raw, high-energy Lagos street-pop pushed into eccentric electronic territory -- a good jolt of fresh regional club sound for a set.
