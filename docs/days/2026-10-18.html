(re)weave

Tristan Arp

New artist discovery on Kapsela, Objekt's label. Connected to your Wisdom Teeth-adjacent (Facta, K-LONE) and experimental club picks.

Experimental ClubTechnoUK Bass

"(re)weave" (April 17, 2026, Kapsela, KAPS005) is a four-track 12" from Detroit-born, London-based producer Tristan Arp -- wiry ostinatos, earthy percussion, field recordings and syncopated kicks, written while moving between Mexico, New York, Detroit and London and inspired by re-reading Borges.

Why listen: Dense, labyrinthine experimental club tracks that reuse and recycle motifs from the artist's past work -- a deeper cut from earlier this year, but a genuinely interesting new entry for a headier or more textural stretch of a set. Research was unusually dry today; flagging the age of this one transparently.
