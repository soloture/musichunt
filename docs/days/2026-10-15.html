"Radical Frame" (September 18, 2026, Ninja Tune) is Darren Cunningham's eighth album as Actress -- eight tracks of fractured, abstract electronics featuring CASISDEAD on "Live By You" and Rainy Miller on the 16-minute closer "Sheets," his first full-length for the label since 2023's "LXXXVIII."

Why listen: Warped, abstracted techno and IDM from one of the UK's most singular electronic producers -- dense, strange material for a headier stretch of a set.
