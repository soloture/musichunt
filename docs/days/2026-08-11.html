ARTIST Label watch Album · Jun 19, 2026

The Trip To Vega

Jeff Mills

New album on his own label, Axis Records.

Techno

Detroit techno originator (Underground Resistance) who's run Axis Records since 1992. "The Trip To Vega" (June 19, 2026) is a conceptual album set in the year 2097, imagining humanity leaving a used-up Earth for another star system.

Why listen: A concept record from one of techno's foundational figures -- still rigorously functional, but with the sci-fi narrative ambition that's defined his solo work for decades.

ARTIST From your sets Compilation · Feb 24, 2026

Ionic (Various 2 comp)

Mogwaa

Already in your library (LGR playlist).

TechnoHouse

South Korean producer, previously seeded with labels Star Creature, Spring Theory, Klasse Wrecks, Gudu, Clique, Zen2000, MM Discos, and his own Walls and Pals. "Ionic" (Feb 24, 2026) is his contribution to Altered Circuits' four-track "Various 2" compilation.

Why listen: Quirky, pointillist hooks over 909 kicks and a rolling sub pattern -- a more straight-up club cut than some of his boogie/disco output, worth knowing if you only had him filed as a boogie producer.
