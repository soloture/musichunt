GET MEAN

Boy Harsher

New artist discovery, first full-length since 2022. Darkwave/coldwave-adjacent territory that can cross into techno-leaning sets.

DarkwaveColdwaveIndustrial

"GET MEAN" (September 18, 2026) is the third studio album from Jae Matthews and Augustus Muller's Boy Harsher -- twelve tracks on grief and renewal, recorded across eight locations in the US, Mexico and Italy, including the singles "Jeans" and "Hard Beat."

Why listen: Icy, pulsing synth-driven darkwave with real club weight -- a strong texture change for a set that wants to go moodier and more industrial without losing momentum.
