ARTIST Label watch EP · Jun 1, 2026

Embalmed In Martino

Lauer

New release on Altered Circuits.

Nu DiscoSynthElectroclash

Philipp Lauer, a three-decade veteran of the Frankfurt studio Pyramide 2 working under numerous aliases. "Embalmed In Martino" (June 1, 2026) is a four-track EP -- Embalmed, Martino, Transactional, Don't You Know -- his tongue-in-cheek ode to the Belgian Martino sauce, moving from early-80s Manchester-style electroclash bass through Miami basslines to soaring synthwave.

Why listen: Playful, synth-forward electroclash/nu-disco with real dancefloor structure -- a good change of pace between deeper house cuts.

ARTIST Label watch EP · Mar 20, 2026

Intro (25th Anniversary)

Alan Braxe & Fred Falke

New release on Smugglers Way.

French HouseDisco

French house originators marking 25 years of their seminal 2000 track "Intro". "Intro (25th Anniversary)" (March 20, 2026) pairs two new remixes -- Falke's cosmic, dubby rework with a new bassline, and Braxe's raw "hotel room edit" -- built on the original's vocal lift from The Jets' "Crush On You".

Why listen: A genuine piece of French house history reworked for today's floors -- useful both as a history lesson and as two genuinely different takes on the same classic to mix into a set.
