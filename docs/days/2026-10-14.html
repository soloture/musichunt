Bestiaria

Karen y Los Remedios

New artist discovery on ZZK Records. Latin electronic/cumbia territory connected to your Nicola Cruz and ZZK-adjacent picks.

CumbiaLatin ElectronicExperimental

"Bestiaria" (October 8, 2026, ZZK Records) is the sophomore album from Mexican "cumbia noir" group Karen y Los Remedios -- twelve tracks of ritualistic, myth-steeped cumbia drawing on figures like water nymphs and witches, following their 2023 debut "Silencio."

Why listen: Dark, hypnotic cumbia rhythms with a theatrical, folkloric edge -- a striking change of texture for a set that wants to move away from house and techno without losing the groove.
