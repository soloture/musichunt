"TON UP" (June 26, 2026, Studio Barnhus) is Gold Panda's (Derwin Schlecker) new full-length, following 2023's The Work -- eight rough-hewn dance tracks and two shorter interludes, led by the single "Ding The Motor," sticking to his sampler-heavy, textured production style.

Why listen: A sampler-warped, dance-leaning side of a producer usually filed under headphone electronica -- good for a textural, rhythmically off-kilter stretch of a set.
