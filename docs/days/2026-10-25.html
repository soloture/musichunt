Decode Vol.2

New artist discovery on Ilian Tape. Connected to your Martinou pick -- also an Ilian Tape-affiliated producer.

Why listen: Heavy, grimy low-end with a breaks backbone -- functional, weighty material for a darker stretch of a set.
