The Rhythm Grooves

ODF

New release on Shall Not Fade.

UK GarageHouseBreaks

Olivier de Friend, a UKG and grooving-breaks specialist stepping into house territory. "The Rhythm Grooves" (August 28, 2026) is a four-track statement EP -- The Rhythm Grooves, Free Your Soul, Back To Mine, Do U Feel Me? -- marking his transfer to Shall Not Fade's main roster after two years of singles on his own On Da Floor label.

Why listen: Classic US garage-house warmth filtered through a UKG producer's ear for groove -- solid, rolling material for a set's warmer stretches.

ARTIST Label watch Album · Jul 24, 2026

LILA

Mohamed Bourouissa

New release on PAN.

AmbientExperimental

Algerian-born, Paris-based photographer, known for using the documentary medium to probe subtle systems of power. "LILA" (July 24, 2026) is his first extended musical work -- 12 tracks on PAN, each named after and inspired by people in his life.

Why listen: Not a club record -- a surrealist, photographer's-ear take on ambient/experimental sound, in the same headphones-first vein as your Patrick Belaga pick from earlier this week.
