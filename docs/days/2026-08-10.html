ARTIST Label watch

Steve Rachmad

New release on Dekmantel Records, watched via your Dazion/Wata Igarashi connections.

TechnoAmbient

Dutch techno pioneer (recording since the late 1980s as Sterac and under his own name) marking 45 years in electronic music. "Light And Time", out August 7, 2026, is his first album for Dekmantel.

Why listen: A deliberate change of direction — orchestral samples arranged in Kontakt, layered over his usual synth/drum-machine language, making it his most ambient record to date. Good for a room that wants texture over drive.
