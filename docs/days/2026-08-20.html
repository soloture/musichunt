ARTIST Label watch EP · Apr 9, 2026

The Perception

Igaxx & Gropina

New release on Paesaggi Records.

PsychedelicAmbient

Tokyo-based Igaxx and Amsterdam-based Gropina on a shared four-track 12" and digital release. "The Perception" (April 9, 2026) pairs Igaxx's original tracks with Gropina's reworks.

Why listen: Near-beatless, spiralling psychedelia on the originals; Gropina's "Version" reworks bring a supernatural, Kraut-inspired lift -- a good pair of headspace tracks for a set's stranger corner.

ARTIST Associated Compilation · Mar 31, 2026

Live at Lux Fragil, 2026

Lena Willikens X Elena Colombi

Lena Willikens is already seeded (Cómeme roster). Elena Colombi runs Osàre! Editions, which this release is on.

ExperimentalElectronic

A recorded B2B DJ set from two well-regarded selectors. "Live at Lux Fragil, 2026" (March 31, 2026) is a limited cassette on Elena Colombi's own Osàre! Editions, recorded at the Lisbon club -- Bandcamp exclusive, no repress planned.

Why listen: Not a studio release but a document of an actual set -- useful less as tracks to cue up and more as a reference for how two sharp selectors sequence a night.
