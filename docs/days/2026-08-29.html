Cuisine (With Piscatorial)

Severed Heads

New release on Dark Entries.

ExperimentalIndustrialElectronic

Australian experimental/industrial pioneers. "Cuisine (With Piscatorial)" (August 7, 2026) is an expanded reissue of their 1991 opus -- its first-ever vinyl pressing, on 2xLP, with a previously unreleased early version of "Seven of Oceans" included.

Why listen: Genuinely new-to-the-world material from a foundational act -- the unreleased track alone makes it worth a listen for anyone into the industrial/experimental lineage behind a lot of modern club music.
