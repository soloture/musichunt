Music To Watch Seeds Grow By already seeded (two earlier editions from this plant-themed cassette series -- Brian d'Souza's Sunflowers and Patricia Wolf's Yarrow -- were already published, caught via dedup check). This is the series' next edition, not yet covered.

AmbientNew AgeField Recording

"Passiflora" (Music To Watch Seeds Grow By 010, shipped around August 12, 2026) is the tenth edition of the label's plant-themed cassette series, from English duo KiF Productions (Will Cookson and Tom Haverly) -- five tracks tracing the passion flower's life cycle, dedicated to Keith McIvor (Optimo) and his Tranquility Mixes.

Why listen: Calm, structured ambient built around a single plant's biology -- good for a slow-build opener or a come-down stretch late in a set.
